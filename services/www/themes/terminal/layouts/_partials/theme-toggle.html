{{/* Theme switcher - light, dark or system (the default), in the footer A set-and-forget preference, so it lives in the footer and leaves the header to navigation. Text labels rather than icons: "system" cannot be misread, where a monitor icon on its own reads as "desktop". Native radio inputs in a fieldset: the browser supplies the arrow-key behaviour and the screen-reader semantics of a radio group, with no ARIA to keep in sync. The selected option is wrapped in brackets, like a CLI picker; every option reserves the bracket space, so switching never shifts the row. js/theme-toggle.ts checks the stored choice on load and applies a change. */}}
