{{/* Theme switcher - light, dark or system (the default), in the footer A set-and-forget preference, so it lives in the footer and leaves the header to navigation. Text labels rather than icons: "system" cannot be misread, where a monitor icon on its own reads as "desktop". Native radio inputs in a fieldset: the browser supplies the arrow-key behaviour and the screen-reader semantics of a radio group, with no ARIA to keep in sync. The legend names the group for assistive tech only; the visible "theme:" is a plain span in the row, because browsers lay a legend out on its own line (WebKit always, whatever the CSS). The selected option is wrapped in brackets, like a CLI picker; every option reserves the bracket space, so switching never shifts the row. System, the default, is checked in the markup, so the group shows a selection before (or without) the script; js/theme-toggle.ts then checks the stored choice and applies a change. The inputs are visually hidden with Tailwind's sr-only, and their labels take the click. */}}
